# Research and risk protocol

Use this reference for transport, borders, entry rules, registration, ticketing, weather, health, safety, and other time-sensitive claims.

## Source hierarchy

1. Government, immigration, embassy, airport, railway, airline, attraction, or municipal primary source.
2. The actual transport operator, hotel, tour operator, or ticketing platform responsible for the service.
3. Reputable secondary reference when the primary source is unavailable or ambiguous.
4. Community reports only as operational color, never as the sole basis for a legal or high-stakes claim.

Cross-check consequential claims when one source does not fully answer the question. Link to the exact supporting page, not a search result.

## Evidence states

- **Confirmed booking:** supplied booking or ticket for the exact date and traveler. Keep the user's times and service number unchanged unless a newer confirmation is supplied.
- **Verified current:** checked against a current primary source. Store the check date.
- **Planning assumption:** plausible but not confirmed for the travel date. Label it clearly.
- **Recommendation:** an editorial choice, not a fact.
- **Unknown:** preserve as unknown; do not guess.

## High-risk checks

For multi-country or repeat-entry trips, verify each entry separately. Check nationality-specific visa eligibility, passport validity, permitted stay, number of entries, registration obligations, border crossing mode, and the effect of domestic and international transfers.

For transport, verify the local date of arrival, airport and terminal, same-ticket versus self-transfer status, baggage handling, connection logic, operating days, seasonal schedules, sales window, fallback routing, and the cost of failure.

For accommodation, distinguish a legal registration obligation from a property's operational practice. Do not promise that a host, hotel, airline, or transit program will provide a benefit without a current source.

For emergency contacts, include only numbers relevant to the itinerary and user context: local police/ambulance/fire where applicable, the responsible embassy or consulate, travel insurer, carrier, mobile operator roaming support, and card-issuer overseas hotlines. Prefer official pages, store the verification date, render phone links with international dialing form, and mark weak or unavailable sources for recheck. Do not copy a broad unverified phone directory into the public bundle. Personal policy or card numbers remain private.

## UI treatment

Keep the main itinerary readable. Put source links, alternative services, fare observations, and legal detail in expandable sections. Place a visible `需临出发前再次确认` badge beside dynamic facts, and include a final booking checklist with an owner and deadline.

## Budget verification

Record the observation date, currency, party-size basis, inclusions, and cancellation terms. Use ranges for unbooked items. Exchange rates and prices are dynamic; identify the assumed rate and contingency rather than claiming false precision.
